Arbolito · Design system · Mix

Ink, glass and fluo.

One token set for the landing, the MiniPay mini app and the public pages. Serif for things you read once and big: headlines and the amount you receive. Sans for everything you act on. Mono for anything that must be exact: rates, IDs, fingerprints. Accent colours are signals, never decoration: pink means live or attention, lime means savings and verified, blue means links and secondary data.

Contrast: body text on bg and surface is at least 7:1 in both themes, and muted text at least 4.5:1. Lime is only ever a background behind ink text (11:1), never a text colour on light. In dark, lime becomes the primary button, with ink text on it. Currency colours: pesos are blue, reais lime, dólares pink. They always sit as a tinted badge with a strong glyph, never as a fill behind body text.

Handoff

Ship the tokens as CSS custom properties on :root and @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark), which works with the current plain-CSS Next.js app. I recommend Tailwind v4 on top (@theme reads the same variables), so components stay inline-styled and consistent. Fonts: Instrument Serif 400, Manrope 400–800 and JetBrains Mono 400–600 through next/font, subset to Latin with display: swap. The 3D coin (three.js) only loads on the landing, and only when prefers-reduced-motion is off.
